## Chapter summary

**Finals**, and the story's **climax**. On **Day 4**, [Slim](../Characters/Slim.md) kidnaps the school:
[Sandman](../Characters/Sandman.md) puts it to sleep with transformed sleeping dust released through the
sprinkler system, and [Bubba](../Characters/Bubba.md) envelops it in a bubble and carries it into the sky.
The player fights Slim in an epic final boss fight in both the real world and the Dream World. Slim's dream
collapses as he realizes the students won't follow him and he's been bested by someone with weaker
powers, and he slips into another dimension, gone for good.

"Finals" means **both**: it's the school's exam week, and it ends in the final battle. The final boss fight
happens on **Day 4**, the same day as the kidnapping. Days 1–3: nothing planned yet `[TBD]`.

### Day 4 — the kidnapping and the final battle

1. **The player wakes himself.** He falls asleep in Sandman's dust like everyone else, but he's aware that
   the school has been put to sleep and something is wrong. He navigates through his own dream and **wakes
   himself up**, a dream-walker skill he learned in **Chapter 3**.
2. **Waking the students.** [Sandman](../Characters/Sandman.md) procured a **dream potion** from a student
   who can make dreams for people in the form of potions `[TBD: who]`, to give the students a taste of what
   their genetically upgraded powers will feel like. The player goes into the students' dreams, **convinces
   them that the new powers won't truly make them heroes**, and wakes them up. They join him to fight
   **Slim's goons**.
3. **Boss stage 1: Sandman.** The former mentor stands in the way of the player stopping Slim. The player
   defeats him.
4. **Boss stage 2: Slim on the school roof.** Key to the fight is the **Mystic Punch**, a technique the
   Gym/combat teacher, **Mr. Brock**, taught the player in a special training session in **Chapter 4**. The punch knocks the target out for a split second, letting the dream-walker **enter their
   mind mid-strike**: this is how the player enters **Slim's mind** for the Dream World half of the fight,
   where Slim's dream collapses.
   - Meanwhile [Bubba](../Characters/Bubba.md) is **in a trance**, straining hard to keep the school afloat
     inside the bubble. Even in the trance he can **observe what's happening around him**: he watches his
     fellow students being heroes despite not having the flashiest powers, and starts to realize that what
     makes a true hero is **what you do with the gifts you're given**. This is his turn against Slim.
5. **Aftermath.** Sandman is **arrested**. Whether he changes his ways is left open, but he's **proud of the
   player for becoming the hero**. The school is brought **back down to the ground** by the turned Bubba and
   [Nimbus](../Characters/Nimbus.md), a second-year student who can turn into a cloud (introduced over the
   course of the year). `[TBD: what becomes of Bubba after]`

### Day 5 — falling action and resolution

- The heroes are **commended** for their action and bravery.
- The students are told that **what they did the day before stands in for their finals**.
- The day ends with the player and his friends **watching fireworks**, looking forward to **summer break**.
