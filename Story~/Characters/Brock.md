## One-liner

The Gym/combat teacher, a former dream-walker whose powers were erased by [Anti Hero](Anti%20Hero.md), and who
teaches the player the Mystic Punch.

## Backstory

He was once a dream-walker. **Many years ago**, [Anti Hero](Anti%20Hero.md) tried to **get into the school and erase
people's powers** with his power-removing ray; Brock **stopped him**, and lost his own powers to the ray. That's why
he **trains students to fight even if their powers won't work**. **What his powers were stays a mystery to the player**
until Ch.4, when he teaches the player the Mystic Punch.

## Relationships

- **To the player:** Teacher; teaches him the **Mystic Punch** in a special training session (**Ch.4**). A fellow
  dream-walker, though the player doesn't know it at first.
- **To others:** His powers were erased by [Anti Hero](Anti%20Hero.md).
