# Bubba

**Role:** Rival / Antagonist
**Status in story:** ✍️ drafting
**First appears:** Ch.1 · Day 1 · Pre (outside the school, after Mike; `1.Pre.SchoolOutside.3`)

## One-liner

The school bully, desperate to transfer to the better school, who resents weaker heroes and later lets
Slim upgrade him to help kidnap Tungsten High.

## Powers / abilities

- **Power class:** [TBD]
- **What he can do:** Makes **small bubbles** around himself and other students, and can use them to **float** himself or
  others in the air.
- **Upgraded (by Slim's machine):** can envelop the **entire school in a bubble** and carry it away into
  the sky.
- **Limits / weaknesses:** His bubbles are **easily poppable**.

## Role in the plot

- **Threads they touch:** [A. Central mystery](../PlotThreads.md#a-the-central-mystery--the-plot-to-kidnap-the-school) ·
  [F. Rivals & enemies](../PlotThreads.md#f-rivals--enemies)
- **Arc across the 6 chapters:** Bully in Chapter 1 → his transfer is denied and his dream dies (Day 5: his dream of getting into Platinum Prep and making his family proud
  crumbles in an earthquake; Mark fights him and the dream breaks) →
  [Slim](Slim.md) persuades him with the promise of upgraded powers, fame, and fortune, and upgrades him
  with the machine → with [Sandman](Sandman.md), he **kidnaps the school**: Sandman puts it to sleep and
  Bubba bubbles it up into the sky. This is the climax, **Chapter 6, Day 4**. Held in a trance keeping the
  school afloat, he can still observe what's happening around him; watching his fellow students be heroes
  despite not having the flashiest powers, he realizes **what makes a true hero is what you do with the gifts
  you're given**, turns against Slim, and helps [Nimbus](Nimbus.md) bring the school back down. Afterward he is
  **forgiven**.
