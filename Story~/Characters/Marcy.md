## Backstory

*About 20 years before the game, seen in flashbacks.*

- A **Tungsten High student**. With Slim and [Sparks](Sparks.md), she worked on a machine that genetically
  upgrades powers for the **school science fair**.
- Slim tested the machine on her. She became a giant werewolf creature, rampaged through the school, and
  was killed by [Anchorman](Anchorman.md), a local hero at the time.
- Her body was found in its **original form**, so **no one knew she was the monster**; everyone thought
  she was a victim of the attack.
