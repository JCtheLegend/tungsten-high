# Mark (the Player / Protagonist)

**Role:** PC (player character)
**Status in story:** 🔨 in game (playable across existing content)
**First appears:** Ch.0 · Dream

## One-liner

**Mark**, a largely silent protagonist: a Tungsten High student who can walk in and out of the **Dream World** — and
*into other people* — and who ends up pulling at the thread of a plot to kidnap his school.

## Powers / abilities

- **Power class:** `[TBD — is dream-walking a recognized class, or unique to him?]`
- **What he can do:**
  - **Dream-walking** — cross into the Dream World and back; transported there each night (`dream` stage).
  - **Walk into people** — enter a **sleeping** person's dream: by **touch** while awake (straight into their
    dreamspace), or while asleep by traveling to their **floating head** in the Dream World. His body goes into a
    **meditative state**. Inside, he can **read memories**, **talk to them** as they act out their dream, or **wake
    them up**. (Full rules: [Worldbuilding](../Worldbuilding.md#the-protagonists-gift-dream-walking).)
- **In combat:** grid-based movement with a **punch** (costs energy) and a **block/shield**; energy
  regenerates over time. (See `PlayerCombat/` — the `Grid Player` root.)
- **Dream World gear:** a **sword** (hidden in his Inner Mind's Bad Area: courage and willpower against fear) and a
  **shield** (hidden in the Good Area: restraint, self-control, and prudence against selfish desires, hedonism, and
  fantasies). Finding them makes him stronger in Dream World combat. Both are **late-game** (Chs.4–5); the shield comes after
  he escapes the Good Area in Ch.3, when he returns there in a later chapter. Experienced dream-walkers can manipulate
  themselves and their environment in the Dream World.
- **Skills learned during the year:** **waking himself up** from a dream (**Ch.3**); the
  **Mystic Punch**, from a special training session with the Gym/combat teacher, Mr. Brock, (**Ch.4**).
  Both pay off in the Ch.6 Day 4 final battle.

## Personality

- **Wants:** To **become a hero**.
- **Fears / flaws:** He **struggles with how his powers have altered his life plans** and placed **additional responsibility**
  on him. Memories of the life that could have been still haunt him. Also a **fear of talking to strangers** (not
  paralyzing, just one most people have), faced in the Day 1 dream.
- **Voice:** **Largely silent protagonist.**

## Backstory

- Lives at home with **both parents**, **Mom** (who drives him to/from school) and **Dad**, and the household cat,
  **Goblin**. **No siblings.**
- He's the **only one in his family with powers**.
- As a child he got **pinched by a crab on the beach**. The memory feeds his Fear (the Day 1 dream Crab).
- His powers were **revealed in high school, a couple of years before the game**. Since then his **old friends and
  former girlfriend have gone off to college**. We don't see them much in the game, but their memories still haunt him
  with a life that could have been. **Everyone at school knows about his power:** you have to disclose your power when
  applying to a power academy.
- He sees **flashbacks** of Slim's and the school's past throughout the game, through his interactions with the [Janitor](Janitor.md), a fellow dream-walker; in them he **plays as the Janitor**. This is revealed in **Chapter 4**.
