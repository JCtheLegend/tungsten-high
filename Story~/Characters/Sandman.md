# Sandman

**Role:** Ally / Villain \
**Status in story:**  ✅ appears in game \
**First appears:** Ch.1 · Day 1 (walks the player to class and gives him the **Planner**, `1.Gym.PickupPlanner`)

## One-liner

Second-year Psych student, nicknamed **"Sandy"**, with the power to make people sleepy with powder: the player's commanding
mentor, who joins Slim because he believes only raw power will make him the leader he wants to be.

## Powers / abilities

- **Power class:** Psychological
- **What they can do:** Makes people sleepy with powder
- **Unstable Upgrade** (from [Slim](Slim.md)'s machine): Knocks people out instantly. Upgraded, his **transformed sleeping dust** can put the entire school to sleep when it's released through the sprinkler system.

## Personality

- **Wants:** To be **seen as a leader and an inspiration**. He likes people to follow him.
- **Fears / flaws:** Believes **raw strength in his power is the only way** to get there, which is why he takes
  [Slim](Slim.md)'s offer of an upgrade.
- **Voice:** **Aspirational and commanding.**

## Backstory

A **second year** with experience navigating the **Dream World**. [TBD]

## Relationships

- **To the player:** Mentor. A fellow **Psych-power student**, so they share Psych class; as an upperclassman he has
  **experience navigating the Dream World**. On Day 1 he walks the player to class and gives him the Planner.
- **To others:** [Slim](Slim.md) (upgrades him; he becomes Slim's ally) · [Bubba](Bubba.md) (partner in the school kidnapping)

## Role in the plot

- **Threads they touch:** [A. Central mystery](../PlotThreads.md#a-the-central-mystery--the-plot-to-kidnap-the-school) · [F. Rivals & enemies](../PlotThreads.md#f-rivals--enemies)
- **Arc across the 6 chapters:** Mentor to the player → wanting to be a leader and an inspiration, and believing only raw power gets him there, he's persuaded by Slim's promise of upgraded powers, fame, and fortune, and upgrades him → with [Bubba](Bubba.md), he **kidnaps the school**: his sleeping dust in the sprinklers puts everyone to sleep, and Bubba bubbles the school up into the sky. This is the climax, **Chapter 6, Day 4**. He's the first stage of the final battle; after the player beats him he's **arrested**. Whether he changes his ways is left open, but he's **proud of the player for becoming the hero**.
