# Slim

**Role:** Antagonist (main villain)
**Status in story:** ✍️ drafting
**First appears:** Ch.1 · Day 0 · Dream (in a flashback, talking with [Marcy](Marcy.md))

## One-liner

A former Tungsten High student who believes weak-powered heroes should be scientifically upgraded to
stand level with the strong. Twenty years after his machine killed his friend, he's back to take over the
school and produce genetically altered hero students.

## Backstory

*About 20 years before the game, seen in flashbacks.*

- A **student at Tungsten High** with a weak power, called **"Paperboy"** in school. With his friends [Marcy](Marcy.md) and
  [Sparks](Sparks.md), he built a machine that can **genetically alter and upgrade powers** as a project
  for the **school science fair**.
- He tested it on **Marcy**, who could shapeshift into a dog. It turned her into a **giant werewolf
  creature** that couldn't control its rage. She rampaged through the school and was killed by
  [Anchorman](Anchorman.md), a local hero at the time. Her body was found in its original form, so no one
  knew she was the monster; everyone thought she was one of the attack's victims.
- Distraught, Slim was determined to fix the machine. In a frenzy he **used it on himself** and
  **disappeared into the 1st dimension**.
- The player sees this backstory through **flashbacks** spread across the game. *How* the player can see
  them is revealed in a later chapter. [TBD: which chapter, and how]

## Role in the plot

- **Threads they touch:** [A. Central mystery](../PlotThreads.md#a-the-central-mystery--the-plot-to-kidnap-the-school)
- **Arc across the 6 chapters:** Hidden in Chapter 1, where the player only sees clues. Over the game he
  uses the machine on Bubba and Sandman to **kidnap the school**: Sandman puts the whole school to sleep
  with transformed sleeping dust released through the sprinkler system, and Bubba envelops the school in
  a bubble and carries it away into the sky, the story's climax on **Chapter 6, Day 4**. The player fights
  him in an epic **final boss fight in both the real world and the Dream World**. His dream collapses
  within the Dream World as he realizes the students won't follow him and he's been bested by someone
  with **weaker powers**. He **slips into another dimension and disappears for good**. He's **revealed in Chapter 3**, during the attempted
  kidnapping of the principal at the school dance.
