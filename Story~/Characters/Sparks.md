## Backstory

*About 20 years before the game, seen in flashbacks.*

- A **Tungsten High student** and a member of the project, with [Slim](Slim.md) and [Marcy](Marcy.md), to
  build the power-upgrade machine for the **school science fair**.
- After the incident she **realized the danger and stopped developing the machine**. She **never saw Slim
  or Marcy again**.
- She **knows Marcy was the monster**, but keeps it secret because she doesn't want Marcy to be remembered as a monster.
