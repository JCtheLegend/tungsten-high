## A. The central mystery — the plot to kidnap the school

> The spine of the whole game. The player is "working to uncover a plot to kidnap the school."

- **The threat:** [Slim](Characters/Slim.md) is trying to **take control of Tungsten High** so he can produce
  **genetically altered hero students**, using a machine he built that genetically upgrades powers.
  The scheme is to **kidnap the school**: he upgrades [Sandman](Characters/Sandman.md) and
  [Bubba](Characters/Bubba.md) with the machine; Sandman puts the entire school to sleep with transformed
  sleeping dust released through the **sprinkler system**, and Bubba envelops the school in a **bubble**
  and carries it away into the **sky**. He recruits with the promise of upgraded powers, fame, and fortune.
  The kidnapping is the **climax of the story: Chapter 6, Day 4**.
  `[TBD: what Slim does with the school in the sky. Tentative idea, not locked: move it around the world
  and take heroes from anywhere.]`
- **The ending:** The player fights Slim in an epic **final boss fight in both the real world and the Dream
  World**. Slim's dream collapses within the Dream World as he realizes the **students will not follow
  him**, and that he's been **bested by someone with weaker powers**. He slips into another dimension and
  **disappears for good**. The final fight happens on **Day 4**, the same day as the kidnapping. The player falls
  asleep in Sandman's dust with everyone else, then **navigates through his dream and wakes himself up** (a
  dream-walker skill learned during the year). Sandman has given the sleeping students a **dream potion** that
  lets them taste their upgraded powers; the player goes into their dreams, **convinces them the new powers won't
  truly make them heroes**, and wakes them to fight Slim's goons with him. The final battle has stages: first
  **Sandman**, the former mentor standing in his way, then **Slim on the school roof**, where the **Mystic Punch**
  (knocks the target out for a split second so the dream-walker can enter their mind mid-strike) is key: it's how
  the player enters **Slim's mind** for the Dream World half of the fight. Bubba spends the battle in a trance,
  straining to keep the bubble afloat; still aware of his surroundings, he watches ordinary students be heroes,
  realizes a true hero is defined by what they do with the gifts they're given, and turns. Afterward Sandman is
  arrested (proud of the player, reform left open), and Bubba and [Nimbus](Characters/Nimbus.md), a second-year
  student who can turn into a cloud, bring the school back down.
  **Day 5** is the resolution: the heroes are commended, Day 4 stands in for the students' finals, and the
  player and his friends watch fireworks before summer break.
- **The moral:** Heroes aren't made by how strong their powers are, but by the **moral character and
  willpower** of those blessed with any level of strength.
- **The antagonist(s):** [Slim](Characters/Slim.md), a former Tungsten High student. He believes heroes with
  weak powers, like himself and Tungsten High's students, can and should be scientifically upgraded so
  they're seen as just as strong. Twenty years ago his machine turned his friend [Marcy](Characters/Marcy.md)
  into a monster who was killed, and he vanished into the 1st dimension after using it on himself (see
  [Worldbuilding → History](Worldbuilding.md#history)). Allies: **Bubba** (works with Slim) and [Sandman](Characters/Sandman.md)
  (becomes his ally).
- **How the player gets pulled in:** `[TBD]`
- **The Dream World connection:** The upgrade changed Slim's power from turning 2-dimensional to going into
  **lower and lower dimensions**, so he can **appear in the Dream World**. The player also sees
  **flashbacks** of Slim's and the school's past in the Dream World. `[TBD: the rest of the connection]`

### Flashbacks

Flashbacks of the school's and Slim's backstory come up throughout the game. The first is the **Day 0**
intro dream: Slim talks with his friend and project partner [Marcy](Characters/Marcy.md) about the
prototype upgrade machine. [Sparks](Characters/Sparks.md) is there as a student, though the player won't
realize it's her until later. Flashbacks cover the events of ~20 years ago: the science-fair machine,
Marcy's transformation and death at the hands of [Anchorman](Characters/Anchorman.md), and Slim's
disappearance into the 1st dimension. **How** the player is able to see these flashbacks is revealed in a later
chapter: they come through his interactions with the school's [Janitor](Characters/Janitor.md), a **dream-walker
perpetually in a state of remembering that fateful day**. In the flashbacks the **player acts as the Janitor** (a student 20 years ago, with a crush on Marcy). This includes the
Day 0 intro dream, set in the school 20 years ago, though it isn't revealed as a flashback until later. The Janitor is
stuck in the memory out of trauma. In later chapters the player sees a **dream-walking indicator** next to him, but he
**walks away** whenever the player tries to dream-walk in (he can **sleepwalk at will**). Eventually it's revealed who he
really is and what the flashbacks are.
The player learns that Marcy was the monster through the flashbacks, with the **final revelation in Chapter 5 or
6**. The Janitor is revealed in **Chapter 4**. `[TBD: which flashbacks come when]`

**Keeping the secret:** [Sparks](Characters/Sparks.md) knows Marcy was the monster, but keeps it secret because she
doesn't want Marcy remembered as one. She **tells the principal** what the envelope means in **Chapter 3**, the week of the attempted kidnapping of the principal.

### The "Bank" thread (Chapter 1, Days 2→4) *(from existing content)*

A throughline already built into Chapter 1 and the strongest candidate for the mystery's first move:
- **The PR assignment:** students help out at a **building in town** on Days 2–4 of the week. The player's is the **Bank**.
- **Day 2** — PR sets a town assignment; an **ordinary tour** (no foreshadowing). The player visits **Garnet Square** and the **Bank**, opens a
  vault, and collects **quarters** from civic buildings (City Hall, Fountain, Library, Post Office, Nurse).
- **Day 3** — [The Hole](Characters/The%20Hole.md), **in disguise**, scouts the bank to learn when it will be empty ("HoleDisguise"). The Bank Guy has forgotten the vault
  code, so the player and [Micycle](Characters/Micycle.md) fetch it from his house.
- **Day 4** — payoff: the **bank-vault heist**. The Hole is told (by Slim's letters) to make a hole in the vault **before the employee comes to work**, so
  **goons** can come in with bags and take the money. But the money **never comes back out** through The Hole. Confused, he
  goes into the vault to investigate. The **goons vanish into another dimension** with the money.
  A goon posted outside the vault takes the player, Mike ([Micycle](Characters/Micycle.md)), and the Bank Guy **hostage** at gunpoint. The Bank Guy hints to the player to enter the vault's **silent-alarm code** (all 1s, planted on Day 3); the goon lets him try, mocks him when it fails, and never realizes the alarm went out. The school
  and police surround the building; hours of stalemate follow, and The Hole realizes he can't escape either.
  A **police megaphone** announces the building is surrounded. Hours later, [Sandman](Characters/Sandman.md) ("**Sandy**") takes the megaphone and tells
  everyone inside to **relax** while he deliberately throws his drowsing powder through the **air vents**, everyone gets sleepy, and the player
  dream-walks into the **goon's mind** to retrieve the **vault code** (the goon had reset the vault to a code only he
  knows): a luxury-lifestyle dream of what he'll spend his cut on, where the player collects the numbers and fights
  the dream goon for the last one. Then he opens the vault and finds The Hole there.
  The player fights **The Hole**. Inside the vault he wonders where the money went and when he'll get his cut, not realizing
  **Slim has disappeared the money and the other goons into a smaller dimension, leaving The Hole to be framed** for the heist.
  The vault money disappears,
  **turned into another dimension**: a clue that someone (Slim, unrevealed) is pulling the strings of the heist.
  The Hole works only for Slim but **doesn't know who he is**; he's guided by **letters that appear out of thin air**.
  Slim needs the money to **work on the machine, hire goons, and finance his plan**.
- **Day 5** — a mysterious, **empty envelope** appears in Sparks' classroom, a clue that someone is watching the school.
  It's Slim's **taunt**, and his message that **he has returned**. Slim was called **"Paperboy"** in school,
  so Sparks recognizes it: she's shocked and frightened, but doesn't tell the class or the player why.
- **The letters** are slipped in from a **lower dimension**. The player may notice that The Hole's letters and
  Sparks' envelope look the same.
- **Aftermath** — The Hole is arrested, put in **power-cancelling handcuffs**, and sent to the **town jail**. The outside
  **goon** is arrested and jailed too. The player can **go talk to him** in jail, which gives clues about who's behind the
  heist: whoever hired him communicated **only via envelope**. He laments being in jail and his ruined dream of getting
  rich. The jail is a building in town the player can enter most times he's walking around town.
- The **Bank Guy** is an innocent employee. He **forgets the vault code** and needs it to get money out, so on Day 3
  the player and [Micycle](Characters/Micycle.md) (his PR partner for the week) go to **his house** to get it.
